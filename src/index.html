What gets checked — and what doesn't

Checks file contents and case-sensitive relative paths, not empty folders, permissions, timestamps, symlinks or cloud-only files the browser cannot read. A matching snapshot is evidence of matching recorded content, not proof of when or where a backup was made. Moved means a unique identical-content pair at different paths; duplicate-content matches stay as added/missing. Scans read files sequentially in 4 MiB chunks. Keep folders unchanged while scanning. Snapshot and report exports contain file names; share them thoughtfully.
